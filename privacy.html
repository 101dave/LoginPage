Privacy Policy — Scholog

Effective Date: October 23, 2026 • Website: https://scholog.vercel.app

1. Information We Collect

Scholog collects only the information necessary to provide class attendance management across your devices:

  • Account Information: Your email address, encrypted password, optional display name, and optional profile image when you create a Scholog account.
  • Class & Attendance Records: Class section names, class schedules/subjects, student names, student IDs, optional gender and email fields, daily attendance statuses, and optional attendance remarks created by the signed-in teacher.

2. How We Use & Protect Your Data

All records are stored securely using encrypted HTTPS connections and Row Level Security (RLS) so that each signed-in account can access only its own class records across devices. QR code scanning runs completely locally on your device camera — video frames are never uploaded anywhere. We never sell, rent, or share user or student data with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Backup, Export & Deletion

Users may export (JSON, CSV/Excel SF2, or PDF), modify, or permanently delete sections, students, and attendance logs directly within the application at any time.
