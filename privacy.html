Privacy Policy — Scholog

Effective Date: October 22, 2026 • Website: https://scholog.vercel.app

1. Information We Collect

Scholog collects only the information necessary to provide class attendance management across your devices:

  • Account Information: Your email address, encrypted password, optional display name, and optional profile image when you create a Scholog account.
  • Class & Attendance Records: Class section names, student names, student IDs, optional student email addresses, and daily attendance statuses created by the signed-in teacher.
  • QR / Student ID Check-In: When a teacher opens a check-in session, students may scan a QR code or type their Student ID on the public check-in page. Only the Student ID, the class section, the date and the resulting attendance status are processed; no account or device information is collected from students.

2. How We Use & Protect Your Data

All records are stored securely using encrypted HTTPS connections and Row Level Security (RLS) so that each signed-in account can access only its own class records across devices. Student check-in links are temporary, tied to a single section and date, expire automatically, and can be closed by the teacher at any time. We never sell, rent, or share user or student data with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Backup, Export & Deletion

Users may export, modify, or permanently delete sections, students, and attendance logs directly within the application at any time.
