    How do I check in?
    1. Scan the QR code on your school ID, or tap Type ID and enter your Student ID.
    2. Scholog checks your ID against today’s class list.
    3. You’ll see a big green confirmation — that means you’re marked Present.

    One phone, one check-in. Each phone can check in one student per session, so a phone can’t be passed around the room. If you share a phone, ask your teacher to check you in from their device.

    Something not working? Ask your teacher to open a new check-in, or try the other option (scan / type).
